### Headline

The GFS Gaussian fixture is **on disk, sha256-verified, and registry-conformant**, but it
**does not yet decode end-to-end**. GDT 3.40 parsing and DRT 2/3 decoding both work. The sole
end-to-end blocker is **Product Definition Template 4.12** (49 of 104 fields), which `parse_section4`
has no arm for, so `decode()` returns `Err` on the first PDT-4.12 field (field 56) with zero fields
decoded. The diagnostic test fails one step earlier because no golden reference exists for the
fixture.

> **Correction carried forward.** Earlier drafts of this umbrella's deliverable attributed the
> failure to GDT 3.40 / N-parameter / DRT-3 handling and marked the fixture "✅ Supported". That
> diagnosis was wrong — those paths work, and PDT 4.8 is absent from the fixture (the fixture splits
> PDT 4.2 / 4.12, not 4.8). The canonical doc records the corrected ground truth (§2, §3b, §4).
